Online Application for Social Security Pension (Form-A)

Applicants for Old Age Pension (IGNOAPS) should have completed 60 years of age. Annual family income should be below Rs. 1,00,000 as per the income certificate issued by the Village Officer. The application is free of cost. No fee is collected from applicants at any stage. Fields marked * are mandatory. Applicants should keep the scanned copy of the income certificate (JPG/PDF, max 1 MB) ready. Use of browser back button is not permitted.
